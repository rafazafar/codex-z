Prototype state and data notes

The first two Accounts in the screenshot show 9% used for 7 days, and 1% used for 5 hours / 0% used for 7 days. The third Account, plans, renewal times, and error scenarios are demo data, not real subscriptions. Renewal times use UTC+8 and are independent of quota reset and reset-card expiry. As requested, Pro 5x / 20x examples have no 5-hour window. A arranges applicable windows in one quota column without empty slots. Applicable quotas not yet received remain unknown, not 0%. Plan labels do not establish product capabilities. Deletion needs confirmation. Switch defaults before deleting the default Account. Refresh, add, default selection, delete, and reset are in-memory simulations only.
